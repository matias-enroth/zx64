zx64 keyboard

The ZX81's legends on the C64 keys. Letters, digits and . are where the C64 has them; everything else is the ZX81's own SHIFT, keyword and function layer. Read from your ROM's key tables.

Find a key

    On the C64 keyboard

    above the key: keyword, at the K cursor top right (red): with SHIFT below the key: function, after SHIFT RETURN bottom right: block graphic, SHIFT in graphics mode dashed: not used

    The cursor tells you what a key gives

    KKeyword

    Start of a line, or after THEN. A letter gives the keyword above it: P is PRINT, K is LIST. Digits give a line number.

    LLetters

    Everywhere else. Keys give their letter; SHIFT gives the red legend: SHIFT P is ", SHIFT L is =.

    FFunction

    SHIFT RETURN, then one key: the function below it. B is INKEY$, T is RND, M is PI.

    GGraphics

    SHIFT 9 in, SHIFT 9 out. Plain keys give inverse characters; SHIFT gives the block graphic in the key's corner.

    C64 keys that zx64 adds

    C64 keyZX81
    RETURNNEWLINE. SHIFT RETURN is FUNCTION
    INST/DELRUBOUT (the ZX81's SHIFT 0)
    CRSR ⇕ CRSR ⇔Down and right; with SHIFT, up and left (the ZX81's SHIFT 6, 8, 7, 5)
    ← ↑Cursor left and up
    SPACE RUN/STOPBREAK while a program runs
    SHIFT LOCKHolds the left SHIFT down, as on the C64
    SHIFT 1EDIT: brings the current line down to be changed
    F1Type-ahead on or off. On (the default), every key is taken however fast you type, as on a C64; off, the ROM's own way: one key at a time, all keys up in between. The top row says which, for 2 seconds
    F2(SHIFT+F1) The colours: black on white, white on black, green screen, amber, black on green, the C64's own. The top row names each; F7, F3 and the monitor keep it
    F3Memory: 16K, 32K, 1K, 16K again. Like swapping the RAM pack with the power off: the ZX81 starts again and the program is gone. The top row says the size
    F5The machine code monitor: stops the ZX81 where it is and shows its registers, on a C64 screen of its own. F5 again, or X, carries on
    F7Reset: the ZX81 starts again, as at power on, when the key comes up. Works at the prompt, in SLOW mode, and between BASIC statements in FAST mode

    Index

    Legends are decoded from the ROM's tables (K-UNSHIFT, K-SHIFT, K-FUNCT, K-GRAPH and the token table), so they match what your zx64 build does. The C64's own + - = : and so on do nothing: use the ZX81's SHIFT legends.
